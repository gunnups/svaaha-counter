    Teach it your voices (optional, recommended)

    Say “svāhā” 5–8 times, a few seconds apart, the way it will be said at the homam. Then record 20 s of the mantra and room chatter without svāhā. Tap Teach svāhā once, say all of them, then tap Finish (starting again replaces the earlier examples).

    Testing

    While listening, the app keeps the last 2 minutes of sound. If it misses a svāhā, tap Save last 2 min of sound under the counter; the .wav file can be used to tune the app.
